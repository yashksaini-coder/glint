Glint is an Electron app in plain TypeScript. Everything runs on the user's computer.

<Pipeline />

## The three parts

| Part | Path | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main | `src/main/` | Runs the SDK's native side. Owns camera permission, the settings file, the clipboard and the `espeak-ng` fallback. |
| Preload | `src/preload/` | The bridge: the SDK's own preload, plus a small `window.glint` API. |
| Renderer | `src/renderer/` | The app itself: screens, gestures, board and signal sources. Sandboxed, with no file access. |

Main and the renderer are separate operating-system processes. The preload runs inside the renderer's process, in its own isolated JavaScript world.

Stop, `Esc` and any error that ends the session all return to `consent` and release the camera. A recoverable problem only pauses the board. The camera is opened in exactly one place, `liveSource` in `signals/presage.ts`, and that is reached only from the consent screen's button.

## Pure logic, thin shell

The parts that need to be right are pure functions with no knowledge of Electron or the camera: the [detectors](/docs/concepts/eyebrow-detector), the [scanner](/docs/concepts/scanning) and the settings sanitizer. That is why they can be tested on a recording in milliseconds.
