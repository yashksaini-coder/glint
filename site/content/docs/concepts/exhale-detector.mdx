---
title: Exhale detector
description: How a slow breath out becomes speech, and why ordinary breathing mostly does not.
---

The detector is in `src/renderer/gestures/exhale.ts`. It watches `breathing.upperTrace`, which rises on the way in and falls on the way out.

## What separates a deliberate exhale

Length and gentleness. On the recording:

| Event | Fall duration | Slope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cued slow exhales | 2.9–6.1 s | 2.4–3.1 units/s |
| Falls during speech | 0.97–2.37 s | 3.7–8.6 units/s |

## How it decides

It fires when the smoothed trace has:

- fallen at least **6 units** from its last peak,
- been falling for at least **2.5 seconds**,
- fallen no faster than **5 units per second**,
- and the user was not talking for more than half of the last second.

After firing, it waits for the next inhale before it can fire again. The fall clock starts only once the trace is 1 unit below the peak, so a plateau before a short fall does not count towards the duration.

## Results

On the recording: **3 of 3 cued exhales, none during speech.** It fired two more times with no cue, at 35.9 s and 132.5 s. The second is plainly a real slow breath out at rest: no detector reading only the breathing trace could reject it.

That is why the protection is in the product: an exhale with no text typed does nothing.

Options and defaults are listed in [Detector options](/docs/reference/detector-options).
