The highlight moves by itself, so you never have to aim. You only choose **when**.

## Scanning

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Rows

    Each row lights up in turn. Select when your row is lit.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Keys

    Each key in that row lights up in turn. Select when your key is lit.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Back to the top

    After a key is chosen, scanning restarts at the top row.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keys

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| A phrase on the top row | Speaks it at once and copies it. Your typed text is untouched. |
| `␣` | Adds a space. |
| `⌫` | Deletes the last character. |
| `↩` | Ends every row. Leaves the row without typing, if you picked the wrong one. |

If you pick a row and choose nothing for two full passes, scanning returns to the rows by itself.

## Why the letters are in that order

Letters are ordered by how often they appear in English, not as on a keyboard. With scanning, every position costs time, so the common letters are reached first. `E` is two steps away and `Z` is eight.

## The bottom strip

The strip shows the signals as Glint receives them: the eyebrow trace with its threshold, the breathing trace, whether you are talking, and your pulse. Pulse is display only.
