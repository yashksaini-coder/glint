title: Gestures
description: How to raise your eyebrows and breathe out so that Glint recognises it.
---

Glint has two gestures. Everything else is a key on the board.

## Raise your eyebrows to select

Raise both eyebrows clearly when the item you want is highlighted, hold for a moment, then **relax completely**.

- It fires while your eyebrows are still up, about a quarter of a second after you raise them.
- You must relax before the next select. One long raise selects only once.
- If you were a moment late and the highlight just moved on, Glint assumes you meant the previous item.

Watch the **Eyebrows** trace at the bottom of the board. Your raise should cross the dashed line. If it does not, raise higher.

## Breathe out slowly to speak

Breathe in, then let the air out gently over about three seconds.

- A normal breath is too short to count. The fall must last at least 2.5 seconds.
- A sharp sigh falls too fast to count.
- It will not fire while you are talking.
- With no text typed, it does nothing, so ordinary breathing cannot make Glint speak an empty line.

<Callout type="warn">
  A slow breath at rest can look exactly like the gesture. On the test recording this happened twice in about two and a half minutes. If you have text typed, it will be spoken.
</Callout>

## Keyboard and switches

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `Space` | Select |
| `Enter` | Speak and copy |
| `Esc` | Stop and turn off the camera |

Many physical assistive switches can be set to send these keys.
