Glint is a switch-access keyboard for people who cannot reliably use their hands or voice. A highlight moves across
a board on its own. You raise your eyebrows to pick what is highlighted, and breathe out slowly to say the sentence
aloud. It needs a webcam and the [Presage SmartSpectra](https://smartspectra.presagetech.com/docs/nodejs) SDK. There is no
special hardware and no Glint account.

<Callout type="warn" title="Early software">
  Glint is a challenge entry at version 0.1. It has not been tested with disabled users, and its gestures were tuned on one
  recorded session of one person. It is not a medical device. See [Scope](/docs/scope) for exactly what has and has not been tested.
</Callout>

![The Glint board with "hello" typed, the letter T highlighted, and live eyebrow and breathing traces below](/img/board.png)

## What you can do with it

- **Type without hands.** Pick letters by row and then by key. See [The board](/docs/use/board).
- **Say a common phrase in two selections.** "Yes", "I need water" and four others sit on the top row, and you can edit them. See [Settings](/docs/use/settings).
- **Speak what you typed.** A slow breath out reads the text aloud and copies it. See [Gestures](/docs/use/gestures).
- **Use a physical switch instead.** Anything that sends `Space` and `Enter` works.
- **Try it with no camera.** A recorded session can drive the app. See [Basic usage](/docs/basic-usage).

## How a gesture becomes speech

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### The webcam sees you

    Frames stay in memory on your computer. The SDK turns them into face landmarks and a breathing trace.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### A detector recognises the gesture

    Small functions watch those signals for [a raised eyebrow](/docs/concepts/eyebrow-detector) or [a slow breath out](/docs/concepts/exhale-detector).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### The scanner picks a key

    An eyebrow raise selects whatever [the scanner](/docs/concepts/scanning) has highlighted.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Glint speaks

    A slow breath out sends the sentence to the speech engine and the clipboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
