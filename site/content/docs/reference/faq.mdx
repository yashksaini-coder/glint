## Is it free?

Glint is MIT-licensed. The SDK needs a Presage key, which has a free tier of 100 credits, or 50 minutes of measurement, at the time of writing.

## Does it work offline?

No. The SDK contacts Presage to check your key and fetch its models. The recorded session works without a key.

## Is my video uploaded?

No. See [Privacy and consent](/docs/privacy).

## Can I use it with glasses?

Eyebrow raises were tested with thick-framed glasses and worked. Blinks did not, which is why Glint does not use them.

## Does it work if I can't move my eyebrows?

Not with the camera today. Glint needs a voluntary eyebrow raise, and that is not possible for everyone. A physical switch sending `Space` and `Enter` works instead.

## Can I use a different language?

Not yet. The board is English-only.

## Can it control other apps?

No. Glint speaks and copies to the clipboard. It does not type into other apps.

## Is it a medical device?

No.
