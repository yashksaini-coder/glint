Start with a recorded session. It needs no camera and no key, and it teaches you how the board moves.

## Try it without a camera

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open the recorded session

    Start Glint and press **Try a recorded session (no camera)**. The board appears, and the traces at the bottom replay a real session.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Pick a row

    Rows light up one at a time, once a second. When the second row (`␣ E T A O ⌫`) is lit, press `Space`.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Pick a key

    Now the keys in that row light up in turn. When `E` is lit, press `Space`. An "e" appears at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Speak it

    Press `Enter`. Glint says the text aloud, copies it, and clears the line.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>
  The replay contains real eyebrow raises and breaths, so now and then the board reacts by itself. That is the recording, not a bug.
</Callout>

## Use the camera

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Read the consent screen and start

    Press **I agree — Start camera**. The camera does not turn on before this.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Get ready

    Face a lamp or window and follow the hints. See [Camera and light](/docs/use/setup). The first session of the day can take up to a minute while the SDK loads its models. The board opens by itself when you are in view.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Raise your eyebrows to select

    Raise them when the row you want is lit, then **relax**. Do the same for the key. See [Gestures](/docs/use/gestures).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Breathe out slowly to speak

    Take a breath in, then let it out gently over about three seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Press **Stop** or `Esc` at any time. The camera turns off and you return to the first screen.
