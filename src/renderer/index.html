Glint · hands-free typing and speech

Before the camera turns on

Glint turns raised eyebrows into "select" and a slow breath out into "speak", read through this computer's webcam by the Presage SmartSpectra SDK.

  • Only the person in front of this screen is read. If more than one face is visible, Glint pauses.
  • The camera starts only when you press Start camera and stops when you press Stop or close the app.
  • Video is processed on this device by the SDK. Glint never saves it or sends it anywhere.
  • Nothing you type or say is stored. Spoken text is copied to the clipboard; that is the only place it goes.
  • SDK telemetry is off. Glint uses no AI service, no analytics and no account.
  • Pulse is displayed only, so you can see you are being read. Glint is not a medical device and makes no health judgements.

Settings

Settings are the only thing Glint stores, in one file on this computer. "Forget everything" deletes it.
