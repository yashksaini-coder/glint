Before the camera turns on

Glint reads your face and upper body through this computer's webcam to turn blinks, raised eyebrows and slow breaths into input.

  • Only you, the person at this screen, are read. The camera starts only after you press Continue and stops when you press Stop or close the app.
  • Video is processed by the SmartSpectra SDK, never written to disk and never sent anywhere by Glint.
  • Recording (off by default) saves numbers only, never images, to the local recordings/ folder.
  • SDK telemetry is off.
  • Glint is not a medical device. It makes no health judgements.
